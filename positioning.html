Chapter 2

Epistemological Position

Interpretivist Approach

This research adopts an interpretivist epistemological position. Interpretivism focuses on understanding how individuals create meanings through their experiences and interactions with others.

This study does not aim to find one universal explanation of romantic relationships. Instead, it explores how different students interpret relationships, being single, and personal value within their social context.

Interpretivism is appropriate because the research question focuses on meanings, experiences, and personal interpretations — not on measuring objective variables.
