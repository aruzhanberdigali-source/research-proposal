Chapter 1

Research Question

How do university students understand the role of romantic relationships in their lives, and how do these perceptions influence their sense of self-worth and identity?

Research Context

This study focuses on undergraduate students at Nazarbayev University. It explores how young adults understand romantic relationships, what relationships mean to them, why some students feel pressure to be in relationships, and how relationship status may influence their identity and self-perception.

The research does not assume that being in a relationship automatically creates happiness or higher self-worth. Instead, it investigates how students themselves interpret relationships, being single, and personal value.

Sub-Questions

  1. What does a romantic relationship mean to university students?
  2. Why do some students consider being in a relationship important?
  3. How does relationship status influence students' perceptions of themselves?
  4. What social factors shape young adults' expectations about romantic relationships?
