Chapter 5

Analytical Framework, Including Positionality

Framework: Symbolic Interactionism

Symbolic Interactionism argues that people create meanings through social interactions. People act based on the meanings things have for them. Meanings develop through interaction, and meanings can change through personal experience.

Applied to this research

A romantic relationship does not have one fixed meaning. For some students, a relationship means emotional support. For others, it means belonging and acceptance. For others still, it means meeting a social expectation.

Social interactions
(family, friends, culture, peers)
↓
Meaning of romantic relationships
↓
Interpretation of relationship status
↓
Identity and self-worth

Positionality Statement

This research recognizes that interpretations of romantic relationships may be influenced by the researcher's personal experiences, cultural background, and social environment. The researcher aims to understand participants' perspectives without judging or assuming that one interpretation is more correct than another.
