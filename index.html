The Meaning of Romantic Relationships Among University Students

How young adults construct identity, belonging, and self-worth

This site presents the first part of a research proposal exploring how university students understand romantic relationships, and how those understandings connect to identity and self-worth. It is built as a set of short chapters — use the menu on the left to move between them.

In one sentence

This project asks not whether relationships make students happier, but how students themselves interpret relationships, singleness, and personal value — and what that reveals about identity and belonging.
