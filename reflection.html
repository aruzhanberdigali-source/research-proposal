What working with AI helped me understand, and what I still don't understand about my research design

What working with AI helped me understand

  • AI helped me move from a general idea about relationships to a social science research question.
  • I understood that my topic is not only about happiness, but about meanings, identity, and social expectations.
  • AI helped me connect my research question with interpretivism and symbolic interactionism.
  • I learned that different students can give different meanings to the same experience.

What I still don't fully understand

  • How to avoid leading students during interviews.
  • How students' cultural backgrounds may influence their understanding of relationships.
