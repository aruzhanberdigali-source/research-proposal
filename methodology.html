Chapter 6

Research Design / Methodology

Approach

Qualitative research.

Method

Semi-structured interviews.

Participants

Undergraduate students at Nazarbayev University. Target sample: 10–15 students with a range of relationship experiences (single, in a relationship, previously in a relationship).

Example Interview Questions

  1. What does being in a romantic relationship mean to you?
  2. Do you think relationship status influences how people see themselves?
  3. Why do some young people feel pressure to be in relationships?
  4. How have your ideas about relationships changed over time?

Data Analysis

Thematic analysis: identifying repeated patterns and meanings across interviews.
