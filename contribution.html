Chapter 3

Research Contribution

Existing Knowledge

Previous research has examined romantic relationships through concepts such as relationship satisfaction, attachment, intimacy, emotional well-being, and identity development. Research on emerging adulthood shows that university years are an important period when individuals explore identity, independence, and close relationships.

Research Gap

Existing studies often focus on relationship outcomes and psychological factors. Less attention has been given to how university students themselves construct meanings around romantic relationships, and why relationship status may become connected to identity and self-worth.

Contribution

This research contributes by exploring students' own narratives and interpretations of romantic relationships. Instead of measuring whether relationships increase happiness, this study examines how students create meanings around relationships and how these meanings influence identity and self-perception.
